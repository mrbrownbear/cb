Our Process

Phase 1 / 4

Planning

  • DiscoveryDiscovery
  • WireframingWireframing
  • ContentContent

Our discovery phase, includes gathering key data, brainstorming internally, and collaborating closely to develop a detailed project plan. To ensure your project is set up for success from the start, we then determine a  competitor analysis, target demographics, and design specifications.

Next, the wireframing phase, is where we develop detailed desktop and mobile wireframes to fine-tune site structure and user flow. To ensure we are all aligned, these are then all shared via Figma for your feedback.

Finally, we move to the content planning stage, where we provide expert copywriting and image sourcing services to make sure your content hits the mark.

Phase 2 / 4

Design

  • ConceptsConcepts
  • Site DesignSite Design
  • InteractivityInteractivity

In the design concepts phase, we present a tailored homepage design through Figma, so you can collaborate with us and share your feedback.

Once approved, the full site design phase begins with the cohesive style across all your web pages, ensuring consistency and flow.

We will also integrate custom  interactive visuals and animations to keep users engaged and bring the site to life.

Phase 3 / 4

Interactive Development

  • DevelopmentDevelopment
  • FunctionalityFunctionality
  • CMSCMS

Once the design is approved, we move onto building the site using HTML/CSS/JS, with each section turned into a custom block, which simplifies content management and updates going forward.

We then focus on bringing the site to life through adding the content, migrating data, and implementing interactive visuals & animations.

After this, extra functionalities will be added, including user accounts, forms, and integrations as required. We take security very seriously, so we implement custom coding, CAPTCHA, antivirus/firewall protection, and follow strict security protocols to ensure the safety of your site.

The site is built with mobile-first responsive in mind, using a custom-coded framework by CreativeWeb where we then integrate a bespoke WordPress theme for minimal bloat, faster loads, and better security.

Phase 4 / 4

Testing & Launch

  • QA TestingQA Testing
  • ComplianceCompliance
  • SEOSEO

After development, we use our QA test for compatibility across major browsers and devices, which covers speed, content accuracy, image quality, accessibility, security, and functionality.

Our team reviews GDPR & CCPA compliance, cookie policies, and accessibility guidelines to ensure your site meets all necessary legal and regulatory requirements.

We handle onsite SEO, covering speed optimisation, Google tools integration, Yoast SEO setup, and implementing a strategic redirect policy.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to build a website?

A simple brochure-style website might take 6-8 weeks, while a more complex site like an eCommerce store could take 12 weeks or more. The time frame for building a website depends on the size, complexity, and specific features you require and considerations such as if branding is required. Our team however can work in an agile way on projects with tight timescales and there are options to launch sites in phases, so if you’re in a rush, do feel free to still reach out.

Our pricing depends on your project requirements, with flexible payments made in phases as the project progresses. Typically, our projects range from £10k ($12.5k) to £120k ($157k), with an average cost of around £15-20k ($20k-26k). We do have a minimum project size of £10k ($12.5k).
